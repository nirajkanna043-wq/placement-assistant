Context & Problem Statement

The Campus Recruitment Bottleneck

Why traditional preparation workflows and black-box tools fail engineering students and placement cells.

1. The ATS Rejection Trap

  • 80%+ of campus resumes are filtered out by automated ATS screeners before a human recruiter reads them.
  • Students are unaware of missing role-critical keywords and domain entity density.
  • Lack of feedback leads to repeated rejections and demotivation.

2. Static & Naive Testing

  • Standard portals score tests on simple percentages, ignoring lucky guesses or careless slips.
  • Zero dynamic difficulty: doesn't adapt to student ability.
  • No explainability: doesn't pinpoint which skill gap yields the highest marginal ROI.

3. Institutional Blindspots

  • TPOs (Placement Officers) manage hundreds of candidates on static spreadsheets with zero predictive foresight.
  • At-risk candidates are discovered only after placement season begins.
  • Lack of automated student cohort clustering and intervention workflows.

Module 01: Information Extraction

NLP Resume Parser & ATS Alignment

Tokenizing unstructured resumes, extracting domain entities, and scoring recruitment alignment.

Text Processing Mechanics

  • N-Gram Tokenizer: Generates unigrams, bigrams, and trigrams to capture multi-word technical concepts like "Binary Search Tree", "System Design", and "REST API".
  • Domain Ontology: Matches against 15+ curated technical skills across SDE, Data Analyst, and Frontend roles.
  • Keyword Density Weighting: Rewards contextual depth and project mentions rather than superficial keyword repetition.

Mathematical Formulations

Confidence = min(95, 35 + (Hits × 18) + (Density × 4) + MultiwordBonus)
ATS_Score = ( Σ [Matched_i / 100 × Weight_i] ) / Total_Role_Weight × 100
  • Automatically detects missing industry keywords required by target job descriptions.
  • Auto-calibrates candidate skill levels with 1-click benchmark resume testing.
Module 02: Adaptive Testing

Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT) & IRT

Modeling genuine latent skill mastery while mathematically isolating slips and guesses.

Why BKT Outperforms Raw Scores

  • Guess Isolation: In a 4-option test, random guessing yields a 25% score. BKT suppresses false skill inflation.
  • Slip Tolerance: High-proficiency students who make occasional typos (slip error) aren't harshly penalized.
  • Latent State: Tracks true candidate knowledge $P(L_t)$ as a continuous probability distribution.

Bayesian Update Equations

P(L_t | Correct) = [ P(L)·(1 - S) ] / [ P(L)·(1 - S) + (1 - P(L))·G ]
P(L_t | Incorrect) = [ P(L)·S ] / [ P(L)·S + (1 - P(L))·(1 - G) ]
Next_Belief P(L_{t+1}) = Posterior + (1 - Posterior)·T
  • Slip Probability ($S$) = 0.10 | Guess Probability ($G$) = 0.25 | Learning Rate ($T$) = 0.15
  • Item Response Theory (IRT): Dynamically branches Easy $\to$ Medium $\to$ Hard questions.
Module 03: Machine Learning & XAI

Placement Classifier & SHAP Waterfall

Calibrated supervised inference with transparent feature attribution and sensitivity simulation.

Calibrated Logistic Classifier

P(Offer) = σ(z) = 1 / (1 + e^-z)
z = β0 + β1·Coverage + β2·Quiz + β3·Roadmap + β4·Interview + β5·ATS
  • Normalized feature vector combining technical coverage, diagnostic accuracy, roadmap velocity, and mock interview score.
  • Threshold bias ($\beta_0 = -2.35$) represents competitive campus selection bar.
  • Categorizes candidates: Tier-1 Placement Ready ($\ge 75\%$), Competitive, or At-Risk.

Explainable AI & "What-If"

SHAP Attribution φ_i = w_i · (x_i - x_mean_i)
  • Positive Drivers (+): Visualizes exact features boosting placement odds (+18% DSA coverage).
  • Risk Detractors (-): Highlights vulnerabilities pulling odds down (-14% System Design).
  • "What-If" Simulator: Lets students test scenarios (e.g. boosting SQL to 85% yields +11% marginal probability gain).

Voice Pipeline & Fluency Analytics

Module 07: Institutional Intelligence

TPO Batch Analytics & K-Means Clustering

Unsupervised clustering for placement officers with automated institutional training recommendations.

Cluster 1: Direct Ready (≥ 75%)

High technical coverage and proven interview readiness.

Intervention: Fast-track to product company interview rounds; schedule advanced mock interviews.

Cluster 2: Core Tech Deficit (55-74%)

Good fundamentals but lacking high-weight domain skills (DSA/System Design).

Intervention: Conduct intensive 2-week coding sprint focused on top weighted gaps.

Cluster 3: Assessment Gap (45-54%)

Moderate self-ratings but low quiz accuracy or uncompleted roadmap.

Intervention: Mandate weekly timed diagnostic quizzes and verified coding submissions.

Cluster 4: At-Risk Placement (< 45%)

Overall readiness below 45%; high risk of missing early placement drives.

Intervention: Immediate 1-on-1 faculty counselling and remedial foundations bootcamp.
